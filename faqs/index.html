FAQs

Questions families often ask.

Clear information about referrals, appointments, therapy and getting started with Cornerstone Kids Speech Therapy.

Do I need a referral?

No. You do not need a referral to see a speech pathologist at Cornerstone Kids Speech Therapy, and families are welcome to self-refer. A GP referral is required if you are eligible and wish to claim Medicare benefits for allied health services under a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan. Please speak with your GP about your eligibility.

What can I expect at my first appointment?

Your initial appointment is held via telehealth with a caregiver. We’ll get to know your child and family, work through your family questionnaire and discuss your child’s developmental history, strengths, milestones, communication and current concerns. We’ll identify priorities and goals, discuss funding, review the service agreement and policies, answer your questions, and talk about next steps and suitable appointment times and locations. If your child has an NDIS plan, please have relevant plan information available.

How long are therapy sessions?

Therapy appointments are billed as a one-hour session. Approximately 45 minutes is generally spent in face-to-face therapy, with the remaining time used for clinical notes, home practice recommendations and relevant follow-up or emails. Session notes are emailed to the family.

Where do sessions take place?

Depending on your child’s needs, location and clinician availability, sessions may take place at home, school or kindergarten, in the community, or via telehealth. Mobile appointments are generally offered across Melbourne’s north-west, with travel typically within approximately 20–30 minutes of Kealba depending on the requested appointment time and existing appointments in the area.

How do I get started?

Complete our online enquiry form or contact us by phone or email. We’ll then arrange a free 15-minute phone consultation to discuss your child’s needs and possible next steps.

Can you see my child at their school or kindergarten?

Yes, where appropriate, subject to clinician availability and permission from the education setting. With appropriate consent, we can collaborate with educators and other professionals to support communication goals across environments.

What happens after the initial appointment?

After the initial telehealth consultation, we’ll discuss the recommended next steps and arrange your child’s first session. Early sessions may include observations, informal assessments or screeners to gather further information and guide therapy. A comprehensive standardised assessment is not always necessary before therapy begins and may be completed later if clinically indicated. Comprehensive assessments can also be discussed at a parent or carer’s request.

Still have a question?

We’re happy to talk through your child’s needs and what getting started could look like.
