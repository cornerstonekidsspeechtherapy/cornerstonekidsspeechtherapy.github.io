A little about us

Connection is where communication grows.

At Cornerstone Speech Pathology, we believe that connection, communication and confidence are cornerstones of a child’s development. Every child deserves to be understood, celebrated and supported in their own unique way.

We take the time to truly get to know each child and family, looking beyond challenges to understand their strengths, interests, personality and individual way of communicating.

Our approach is neurodiversity-affirming, respectful and collaborative. We create a warm, supportive space where children feel safe to be themselves, build confidence and develop communication skills that are meaningful to their everyday lives.

Development is about more than achieving milestones. It is about laying strong foundations for connection, confidence, curiosity and participation, while empowering children to communicate in ways that feel right for them.

Meet Melissa

Melissa is an experienced Speech Pathologist and Hanen Certified Clinician who is passionate about supporting children to develop communication skills while feeling confident, understood and empowered to be themselves. With experience across a range of settings and diverse communication needs, she brings a flexible and individualised approach to her work.

Melissa holds a Master of Speech Pathology from La Trobe University and is a Certified Practising Member of Speech Pathology Australia.

FAQs

Some helpful details.

What ages do you support?

Cornerstone Speech Pathology supports children aged 2–12 years.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions may be held at home, at your child’s school or kindergarten, or online via telehealth.

What areas do you travel to?

We support families across Melbourne’s north-western suburbs, generally within a 20–30 minute radius of Kealba. Availability depends on session time and existing appointments in the area.

Do you offer assessments?

Yes. Comprehensive speech and language assessments and screeners are available, along with tailored reports.
