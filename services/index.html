Our Services

Individualised support for growing communicators.

Cornerstone Kids Speech Therapy provides neurodiversity-affirming speech pathology support for children aged 2–12, shaped around each child’s strengths, communication needs and everyday life.

01

Assessments & Screeners

Comprehensive speech and language assessments, observations and screeners can help us understand your child’s communication strengths, needs and profile. The type of assessment used depends on the individual child and the purpose of assessment.

02

Ongoing Therapy

Individualised, engaging therapy connects with your child’s interests, family priorities and everyday communication. Support may be provided at home, school or kindergarten, in the community, or via telehealth where appropriate.

03

Tailored Reports

Clear, individualised reports can communicate relevant assessment findings, progress and recommendations when required or recommended.

What to expect

Our Process

Every child’s pathway is individual. These steps provide a general guide to getting started and continuing support.

A brief conversation to learn about your child, hear your concerns and discuss the most appropriate next steps.

A telehealth appointment to explore your child’s history, strengths, development, communication and your family’s priorities in more detail.

Where appropriate, observations, informal assessments or screeners are completed with your child to better understand their communication profile and guide support.

When recommended or requested, a tailored report can outline assessment findings, your child’s communication profile and relevant recommendations.

Individualised therapy is shaped around your child’s strengths, interests, communication needs and family priorities.

Progress is reviewed over time, with ongoing collaboration between your family and, where appropriate, educators and other professionals.
